Student Support Hub

A confidential space to report what you're going through — academic stress, mental health, bullying, or anything else — and get connected with a PNGUOT counsellor. Submit your case, track its status, and request a session, entirely online.

New Support Request → How it works

Open to enrolled students of Papua New Guinea University of Technology.

How it works

Four steps from submission to resolution. A counsellor reviews every case personally.

1
Submit your request
Fill out a confidential form describing your situation — a case number is generated automatically.
2
Counsellor reviews
Your case is triaged by urgency and reviewed by one of PNGUOT's counsellors.
3
Session scheduled
You can request a time to talk, or a counsellor will reach out to arrange one.
4
Case resolved
Your case stays tracked and confidential until it's closed.
